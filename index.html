🔄 How Recycling Works

Journey from scrap to useful material.

“कचरा नहीं, संसाधन है — सही राह मिले तो निधान है।”
1) Collection & Sorting: Plastic / Glass / E-waste / Metal अलग.
2) Cleaning: Wash, de-label, remove contaminants.
3) Processing: Melt / crush / repulp / extract metals (e-waste).
4) Manufacturing: Pellets / sheets / ingots → new products.

🌍 Where It Goes & How It’s Used

Recycled material replaces virgin resources — saving energy & emissions.

“कचरे को सोना बनाओ, धरती का आभार जताओ।”
  • Plastic: Pellets → buckets, benches, bins.
  • Glass: Cullet → jars, tiles, construction mix.
  • E-waste: Copper/Al/rare metals → new components.
  • Metal: Ingots → appliances, tools, auto parts.
  • Paper: Repulp → packaging, notebooks, tissue.
